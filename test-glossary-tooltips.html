Glossary Tooltips Test

Test 1: Basic Terms

The Tutsi population was targeted by Hutu extremists during the genocide. The Interahamwe militias were organized by Hutu Power ideologues who used RTLM radio to spread propaganda.

Test 2: Organizations and Places

UNAMIR peacekeepers in Kigali were commanded by General Dallaire. The RPF advanced from Uganda while the Arusha Accords collapsed. Many sought refuge at the Hôtel des Mille Collines.

Test 3: Legal Terms

After the genocide, gacaca courts processed over a million cases. The ICTR in Arusha prosecuted senior organizers. The Genocide Convention obligated intervention, but UNAMIR's mandate restricted action.

Test 4: People and Concepts

President Habyarimana's plane was shot down on April 6, 1994. Prime Minister Uwilingiyimana, a Hutu moderate, was killed the next day. The concept of Responsibility to Protect emerged partly from Rwanda's lessons.

Test 5: Compound Terms

The gacaca courts used Category Two classifications for direct perpetrators. A communal secretary had access to population records. The city of Butare and Gitarama saw different patterns of violence than Kigali.
